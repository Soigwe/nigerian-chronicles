**Why it matters:** This is a stealth inflation vector hitting household budgets without any political accountability. The public pays more while the rules quietly changed in the fine print.

## 🌍 WORLD & MACRO IMPACT

### Why Decisions Made in Washington & Vienna Decide the Price of Groceries in Lagos

**Your purchasing power is tethered to two rooms you'll never visit.**

Most people think Nigerian inflation is purely domestic. It's not. Your daily cost of living is heavily driven by:

1. **The US Federal Reserve (Washington):** When the Fed cuts interest rates, global fund managers pull money out of low-yielding US bonds and hunt for high returns in emerging markets like Nigeria. This floods the CBN with USD, easing Naira pressure → lower import costs → cheaper food.

2. **OPEC (Vienna):** When OPEC caps oil production, crude prices rise above $80/barrel. Nigeria earns over 85% of foreign revenue from oil — high oil prices mean more dollar ammunition to defend the Naira.

3. **The grocery shelf:** A stronger or stable Naira immediately lowers the import cost of wheat, fertilizer, packaging materials, and diesel — which is what prevents the next price jump at your local market.

**Bottom line:** If you understand global interest rate cycles and OPEC quota decisions, you can predict the Naira's direction months before the CBN acts.
